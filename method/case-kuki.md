# Case study: Kuki Shūzō, *The Problem of Contingency*

The first vector translation. A summary of what was done, for comparison with later runs. The project is listed on the [Vector Humanities site](https://vector-humanities.github.io/); its repository is private while the translation is checked.

## Text
- Kuki Shūzō (1888–1941), 偶然性の問題 *Gūzensei no mondai* (Tokyo: Iwanami Shoten, 1935), a book-length study of contingency in its categorical, hypothetical and disjunctive forms. No complete English translation had been published.
- Translated from the 1948 fourth printing, National Diet Library Digital Collections, public domain in Japan. 331 printed pages, 174 image spreads.

## Run
- 20–21 October 2026.
- The coordinator translated the Introduction itself as the pilot; the translator set the register and the first glossary.
- Chapters 2 and 3 (with the Conclusion) went to two batches of seven parallel parts, each producing a literal and a readable version with editor's notes.
- A terminology pass followed the existence/being decision (rule 4), with checkers comparing every occurrence against the images.
- Seven read-through checkers compared readable against literal: 106 fixes applied.
- External check of about twenty passages quoted from the 1966 French translation in the secondary literature; a concordance with the *Collected Works* (vol. 2) from 46 anchors.
- Apparatus: Preface from a sourced fact sheet, Translator's Note, four appendices (translation choices, contents of the 1948 printing, concordance, index of persons and works), glossary; 22 figures cropped from full-resolution scans with English keys.
- Book: three design samples, one chosen; a 250-page book built with Pandoc and Typst.

## Output
- Readable translation of about 96,000 words with about 470 translator's notes; a literal version, sentence by sentence, for quotation and checking.

## Cost
- Background agents used about 4.8 million tokens, about 3.9 million of them for translation. Work in the coordinating session was not counted. Later runs should record costs per stage (method §10).

## Open
- Not yet checked against the *Collected Works* or against the French in full.
- Some note attributions unverified; an attribution pass (method §7.3) is due.
- The translator read samples and particular passages, not every sentence against the image. The sample review in method §10 was not run.
