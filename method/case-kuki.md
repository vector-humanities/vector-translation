# Case study: Kuki Shūzō, *The Problem of Contingency*

The first vector translation. A summary of what was done, for comparison with later runs. The project is listed on the [Vector Humanities site](https://vector-humanities.github.io/); its working repository is private, and the translation, the literal translation and the book are on the public [research bench](https://vector-humanities.github.io/kuki-bench/).

## Text
- Kuki Shūzō (1888–1941), 偶然性の問題 *Gūzensei no mondai* (Tokyo: Iwanami Shoten, 1935), a book-length study of contingency in its categorical, hypothetical and disjunctive forms. No complete English translation had been published.
- Translated from the 1948 fourth printing, National Diet Library Digital Collections, public domain in Japan. 331 printed pages, 174 image spreads.

## Run
- 20–21 October 2026.
- The coordinator translated the Introduction itself as the pilot; the translator set the register and the first glossary.
- Chapters 2 and 3 (with the Conclusion) went to two batches of seven parallel parts, each producing a literal version and a translation with editor's notes.
- A terminology pass followed the existence/being decision (rule 4), with checkers comparing every occurrence against the images.
- Seven read-through checkers compared the translation against the literal: 116 fixes applied (the run's log summed them as 106; its per-range figures, 17, 39, 19 and 41, give 116).
- External check of about twenty passages quoted from the 1966 French translation in the secondary literature; a concordance with the *Collected Works* (vol. 2) from 46 anchors.
- Apparatus: Preface from a sourced fact sheet, Translator's Note, four appendices (translation choices, contents of the 1948 printing, concordance, index of persons and works), glossary; 22 figures cropped from full-resolution scans with English keys.
- Book: three design samples, one chosen; a 250-page book built with Pandoc and Typst.

## Output
- Translation of about 98,000 words with 480 translator's notes, and a literal version, sentence by sentence; both on the research bench (rule 21).
- A 238-page book, front matter in roman numerals, built with Vector Book.

## Cost
- Background agents used about 4.8 million tokens, about 3.9 million of them for translation. Work in the coordinating session was not counted. Later runs should record costs per stage (method §10).

## November 2026
- **Capitals for three pairs** (9 November): 根據 Ground and 理由 ground, 邂逅 Encounter and 遭遇 encounter, 樣相性 Modality and 樣相 modality; 217 edits on 66 pages, after agents had read every page image and matched each of these English words to its Japanese.
- **Page reading** (10 November, method §7.6): ten readers read both versions against the images and found 28 errors of meaning, 31 queries, 180 places where one English word stood for two terms and 101 departures from the glossary; 86 edits on 26 pages.
- **Shared words separated** (10 November): 451 edits on 157 pages (基礎づける found, 基く rest on; 契機 moment, 瞬間 instant; 規定 determination, 限定 delimitation, 決定 decision; free-standing 偶然 the contingent, 現實 the actual), 52 proposals dropped; then 71 edits separating 必然, 可能, 不可能 from their -性 forms. "Determine" still renders both 規定 and causal 決定, and the Translator's Note lists the words that remain shared.
- **Positional reading** (12 November): macOS Vision read all 331 pages, a box for each character, with IIIF annotations on the library's canvases (method §2).
- **Notes** (27 November): fourteen notes, chosen by the translator, checked on the full-resolution images and against primary sources; a wrong page number (Poincaré) and two digrams printed reversed were corrected.
- **Book** (27 November): Translator's Preface, Translator's Note and Chronology in roman numerals; an epigraph from Kuki's gravestone; the unchecked concordance taken out of the book and kept in the repository.

## Open
